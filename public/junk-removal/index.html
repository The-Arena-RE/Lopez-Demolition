Overview

When a few trips to the dump won't cut it.

Most junk removal services are built around a single truckload. That works for a couch or a garage corner. It doesn't work when an entire backyard is buried, when years of trash have piled up behind a building, or when a property has been used as a dumping ground.

Lopez Demolition Company focuses on the larger end of the job. We handle bulky waste, heavy debris, and high-volume cleanups that need real labor and real equipment to move.

Tell us what's there and roughly how much of it. We'll talk through access, the type of material, and what it will take to haul it out.

The Work

What we haul

  • Large piles of household junk and bagged trash
  • Bulky items: furniture, mattresses, appliances, and old fixtures
  • Scrap, broken materials, and discarded building debris
  • Yard junk, old fencing, and accumulated outdoor clutter
  • Mixed debris spread across an entire property

Hazardous materials such as chemicals, asbestos, and contaminated waste need special handling. Tell us on the phone if you suspect any are on site so we can discuss what applies.

FAQ

Junk & Trash Removal questions

Anything specific to your property? Call and ask.

How much junk is too much?

Large volumes are exactly what we focus on. Call and describe the job — a pile, a yard, or a full property — and we'll talk through what's involved.

Do you take heavy or bulky items?

Yes. Bulky items like furniture, mattresses, appliances, and heavy debris are a normal part of our work.

How is junk removal priced?

Pricing depends on the amount and type of material, labor, equipment, access, hauling distance, and overall scope. Call to discuss a quote.

Can you remove trash from a vacant lot?

Yes. Clearing dumped trash and debris from vacant land is a common job. See also our land clearing and lot cleanup service.
