Mobile Home Removal

Get That Old Mobile Home Off Your Property.

Unwanted, damaged, or deteriorating mobile homes take up land you could be using. We handle mobile home removal projects and the cleanup that comes with them.

Overview

An old mobile home is more than an eyesore.

A mobile home that's no longer livable can block plans for the land it sits on. It may be falling apart, filled with left-behind belongings, or simply in the way of what comes next.

Lopez Demolition Company takes on mobile home removal jobs along with the debris and site cleanup involved. The goal is the same every time: get the unwanted home gone and leave you with usable ground.

Every mobile home is different. Before we can lay out next steps, we need to understand its condition, where it sits on the property, how equipment can reach it, and what else needs to go with it.

The Work

What we'll ask about

  • The home's overall condition — intact, damaged, or deteriorating
  • Size and approximate age, if known
  • Location on the property and how equipment can reach it
  • Contents still inside and any debris around it
  • Additional structures nearby: skirting, decks, steps, sheds

This service is removal and cleanup of unwanted mobile homes — not transport or relocation of intact homes. If permits, utilities, or hazardous materials are involved, bring them up on the call so we can discuss what applies to your situation.

Before you call

Have these details handy

  1. The property's location
  2. The home's condition and approximate size
  3. How a truck or equipment can access it
  4. Whether contents or extra structures need to go too

FAQ

Mobile Home Removal questions

Anything specific to your property? Call and ask.

Do you remove old mobile homes?

Yes. Contact us with the location, condition, and accessibility of the mobile home so we can discuss removal options and the project requirements.

Do you move intact mobile homes to a new location?

Our service is removing unwanted mobile homes and cleaning up the site, not transporting homes for reuse.

Can you clean up the site after removal?

Yes. Mobile home removal projects include the related debris and site cleanup. Call to talk through the scope.

How much does mobile home removal cost?
